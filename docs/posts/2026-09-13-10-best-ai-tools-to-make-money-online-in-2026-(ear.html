Jasper remains an industry leader for digital marketers, copywriters, and agency owners. It allows you to generate SEO-optimized blog posts, ad copy, email campaigns, and landing page content in seconds.

2. HeyGen – Faceless Video & Avatar Creation

Video is king in 2026, but not everyone wants to be on camera. HeyGen lets you create hyper-realistic AI avatars that speak multiple languages with perfect lip-syncing and natural voice tones.

  • How to Monetize: Build a network of faceless video channels on YouTube and TikTok. Create educational tutorials, product reviews, or local business promo videos. Sell custom AI video avatars to corporate clients for internal training or marketing campaigns.

4. Midjourney v6 – Premium AI Art & Graphic Design

Midjourney continues to set the standard for photorealistic image generation, vector art, and brand assets.

5. Chatbase – Custom AI Chatbots for Businesses

Businesses are eager to automate their customer support and lead generation. Chatbase allows you to upload any business data (PDFs, website links, documentation) and build a custom ChatGPT-style bot tailored to that brand in less than 30 minutes.

Descript revolutionizes audio and video editing by allowing you to edit media files by editing text transcripts. It features automated filler-word removal, eye-contact correction, and AI voice cloning.

7. Opus Clip – AI Short-Form Content Repurposing

Short-form content on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts drives massive viral traffic in 2026. Opus Clip uses AI to analyze long YouTube videos or podcasts, pick out the most engaging hooks, extract the best clips, and automatically add animated captions.

  • How to Monetize: Run a "Content Repurposing Agency." Charge YouTubers and podcasters $1,500/month to turn their long-form videos into 30 viral shorts every month.

8. ElevenLabs – Ultra-Realistic AI Voice Generation

ElevenLabs generates human-like speech with full control over emotion, accent, and inflection. It sounds virtually indistinguishable from real voice actors.

  • How to Monetize:
  • Narrate audiobooks for self-published authors on platforms like ACX (Amazon).
  • Create commercial voiceovers for local radio/TV ads or online video creators.
  • Produce multi-lingual voice tracks for content creators looking to translate their videos globally.

9. Surfer SEO – AI Search Engine Optimization

Surfer SEO analyzes top-ranking Google content and tells you exactly which keywords, headings, and structure to use to rank #1. Coupled with its built-in AI writer, it is a powerhouse for organic search growth.

  • How to Monetize: Rank niche affiliate blogs rapidly to capture organic traffic, or offer SEO optimization audits and content writing to enterprise clients.

10. Notion AI – Digital Product & Template Creation

Notion AI seamlessly integrates generative writing, task management, and data organization inside your workspace.

  • How to Monetize: Use Notion AI to draft, structure, and package digital planners, second-brain dashboards, and business operating systems. Sell these templates on platforms like Gumroad or Etsy for passive income.
