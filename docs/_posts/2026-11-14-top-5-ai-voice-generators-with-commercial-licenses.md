## What You Must Check Before Buying a Commercial License (2026 Checklist)

Not all "commercial licenses" are created equal. Before putting down your credit card, ensure your platform of choice covers these legal baseline protections:

1. **Perpetual Usage Rights:** Ensure that any audio generated while your subscription is active remains legally yours to use *even if you cancel your plan later*. (All 5 tools reviewed above support this).
2. **Explicit Monetization Protections:** Verify that YouTube Content ID, Spotify Royalty streams, and broadcast TV rights are explicitly mentioned in the Terms of Service.
3. **Ethically Sourced Voice Models:** Using AI voice generators built on scraped, unconsented voice actor data can lead to legal complications. Top-tier tools like ElevenLabs and Murf compensate voice actors directly, shielding enterprise clients from legal liabilities.
