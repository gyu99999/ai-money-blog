## 1. Opus Clip: Best Overall for Long-Form Repurposing

If you already produce podcasts, interviews, or long-form YouTube videos, **Opus Clip** is the fastest ROI tool on this list. It analyzes your long video, extracts the most engaging hooks, re-frames the speakers to 9:16 vertical video, and adds styled animated captions automatically.

### Key Features for Shorts Creators
* **AI Virality Score:** Rates clip potential from 0 to 99 using predictive engagement algorithms.
* **Active Speaker Detection:** Automatically centers face cams without manual crop keyframing.
* **Auto-B-Roll & Captions:** Automatically inserts context-aware B-roll visuals and animated captions in popular styles (Alex Hormozi style).

### Pricing Breakdown
* **Free Plan:** 60 processing minutes/month (watermarked).
* **Starter Plan:** $9/month for 150 processing minutes.
* **Pro Plan:** $29/month for 360 processing minutes, custom brand templates, and 4K export.

### Pros & Cons
* **Pros:**
  * Insanely fast—turns a 1-hour podcast into 10–15 Shorts in under 5 minutes.
  * Captions are remarkably accurate out of the box.
  * Directly pushes clips to YouTube Shorts schedule.
* **Cons:**
  * Less useful if you don't already have long-form video footage.
  * B-roll generation can occasionally feel generic on niche topics.

**The Verdict on Opus Clip:** If you produce long-form content, buying Opus Clip is a no-brainer. It pays for itself by turning single long videos into weeks of content.

## 3. Descript: Best for Script-Based & Voice-First Content

**Descript** handles editing like a word document. Upload your footage, and Descript automatically generates a text transcript. Delete a word from the text transcript, and it cuts that exact audio and video section out seamlessly.

### Key Features for Shorts Creators
* **Filler Word Removal:** One-click removal of all "ums," "ahs," and awkward pauses to create ultra-tight, fast-paced Shorts pacing.
* **Studio Sound:** AI-powered background noise removal that turns cheap phone mics into studio-grade audio.
* **Eye Contact Correction:** Uses AI to adjust your gaze so it looks like you are looking directly into the camera lens, even if you were reading a script.

### Pricing Breakdown
* **Free Plan:** 1 hour of transcription/month, 720p export.
* **Hobbyist Plan:** $12/month – 10 hours transcription, 4K export, watermark-free.
* **Pro Plan:** $24/month – 30 hours transcription, advanced AI features (Studio Sound, Eye Contact).

### Pros & Cons
* **Pros:**
  * Script editing makes cutting down talking-head Shorts effortless.
  * Studio Sound feature eliminates the need for expensive audio post-production.
  * Excellent for cross-platform repurposing.
* **Cons:**
  * Steep learning curve compared to simple prompt-based generators.
  * Desktop application can be resource-heavy on older hardware.

**The Verdict on Descript:** Best for creators who record themselves talking to camera and need fast cleanup, tight audio, and flawless transcript editing.
