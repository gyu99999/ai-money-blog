Shopify’s AI auto-generates high-converting product descriptions, auto-segments your customer email lists, and optimizes product placement based on real buyer behavior analytics. Its 1-click **Shop Pay** checkout converts up to 50% better than standard checkouts.

### Pros & Cons
* **Pros:**

  * Seamless integration with all major ad channels and affiliate networks.
* **Cons:**
