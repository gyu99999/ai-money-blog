* ✅ **Native Workspace Intelligence:** Notion AI contextualizes answers using your entire workspace database, letting you query private company documents seamlessly without manual copy-pasting.

* ❌ **Ecosystem Lock-In:** ChatGPT Plus operates in a standalone chat interface, requiring manual effort or complex webhooks to push content into your daily workspace tools.
