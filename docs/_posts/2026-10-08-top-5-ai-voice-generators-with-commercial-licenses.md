## What You MUST Look For in an AI Voice Commercial License

Before pulling out your credit card, make sure your prospective tool ticks these three non-negotiable boxes:

1. **Perpetual Royalty-Free Rights:** Ensure that any audio generated while your subscription is active remains legally yours *forever*, even if you cancel your plan later.
2. **Resell and Client Rights:** If you run an agency, your license must explicitly permit you to transfer the audio rights to third-party clients without paying additional fees.
3. **No Forced Attribution:** Free tiers often require you to put "Created with [Platform]" in your description. Paid commercial plans must completely remove this requirement.
