## How to Choose the Right Commercial AI Voice Generator (Avoid Copyright Lawsuits)

When choosing an AI voice generator for commercial projects, don't focus solely on sound quality. Consider these crucial factors before entering your credit card details:

### 1. Explicit Commercial Usage Rights in Terms of Service (ToS)
A platform claiming "Free Commercial Use" often hides restrictions in its fine print—such as banning paid media advertising, broadcast television, or reselling audio to clients. Ensure the tool grants a **broad, royalty-free, perpetual commercial license**.

### 2. Character Caps vs. Unlimited Generation
If you are producing audiobooks or daily YouTube videos, standard character limits (e.g., 100,000 characters/month) will deplete quickly. For high-volume production, prioritize tools with high monthly limits or unlimited plans like **[Play.ht](https://partnerstack.com/)**.

### 3. Voice Cloning Legality & Security
If you plan to clone your own voice or a hired voice actor's voice, ensure the software platform requires explicit consent verification. Top-tier tools like **[ElevenLabs](https://partnerstack.com/)** feature strict safety protocols to guarantee your cloned voice remains secure and exclusive to your account.
