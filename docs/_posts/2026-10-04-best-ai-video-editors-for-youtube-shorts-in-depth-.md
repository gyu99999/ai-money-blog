## 3. Descript: Best for Script-First Creators & Podcast Highlights

**Descript** revolutionizes video editing by turning your video file into a text document. To edit the video, you simply edit the transcript. Delete a word, phrase, or filler word ("um", "uh") from the text, and Descript cuts it from the video automatically.

For creators who script their YouTube Shorts line-by-line, Descript provides surgical precision over pacing, audio clarity, and visuals.

### High-Converting Features
* **AI Eye Contact Fixer:** Automatically adjusts your eyes to look directly into the camera lens, even if you were reading off a script or teleprompter.
* **Studio Sound:** One-click background noise removal that makes basic smartphone mic audio sound like a $500 studio microphone setup.
* **Text-Based Trimming & Filler Word Removal:** Deletes awkward pauses and repetitive words instantly to keep video pacing lightning-fast.

### Pros & Cons
