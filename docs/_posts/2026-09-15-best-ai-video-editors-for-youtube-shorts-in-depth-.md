## Key Buyer Features: What to Look for Before Upgrading

Before pulling out your credit card, ensure the AI editor you choose checks these three retention boxes:

1. **Auto-Dynamic Captions:** Over 75% of viewers watch Shorts on mute. If your editor doesn't highlight words in real-time with customizable fonts and emojis, your retention rate will plummet.
2. **Auto-Reframing (16:9 to 9:16):** Look for AI visual tracking that keeps your face or subject in the center automatically without manual keyframing.
3. **Pacing Control / Jump Cut Automation:** High-performing YouTube Shorts rely on tight cuts. The software must automatically eliminate dead space at the start and end of sentences.
