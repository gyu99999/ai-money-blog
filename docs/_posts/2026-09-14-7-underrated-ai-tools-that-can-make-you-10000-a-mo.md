### How to Monetize It ($10k/Month Blueprint):
Launch a **short-form content agency**. Charge 5 clients $2,000 per month each to repurpose their long-form video content into 30–60 monthly Shorts/Reels/TikToks. Opus Clip handles 80% of the editing workload in minutes.

* **Pros:**
  * Drastically cuts video editing time by up to 90%.
  * Automatically detects viral hooks using predictive analytics.
  * Includes auto-captioning and visual speaker-tracking.
* **Cons:**
  * Fine-tuning context cuts still requires human oversight.
  * Free tier has render caps; paid plans required for agency volume.
